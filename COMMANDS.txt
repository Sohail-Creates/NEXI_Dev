NEXI SERVICE COMMANDS — COMPATIBILITY POINTER

The authoritative Windows PowerShell startup guide is run.txt at the repository
root. It uses one isolated `venv` per service, CA-verified HTTPS, the current
authoritative ASGI entrypoints, and the real health endpoints.

Do not use historical shared-venv, direct `python main.py`, plaintext HTTP, or
`test_nexi_system_enhanced.py` instructions. That harness is retained only as a
design-reference artifact and is excluded from the protected regression gate.

Current protected test commands and architecture are documented in README.md and
ARCHITECTURE.md.
